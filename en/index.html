Our research

Four themes, one goal: care tailored to each sex

Our laboratory brings together clinical research with patients and basic science research.

1

Understand

The differences between women and men in how heart valve disease develops.

2

Diagnose better and earlier

Criteria, biomarkers and imaging tools tailored to each sex.

3

Treat better

The right timing and the right type of intervention for women, and follow-up of outcomes after treatment.

4

Beyond the valves: the myocardium

The differences between women and men in hypertrophy, fibrosis and heart failure.

The Chairholder

Dr. Marie-Annick Clavel

A full professor in the Department of Medicine at Université Laval and a scientist at the Quebec Heart and Lung Institute since 2014, she holds the Canada Research Chair in Women's Valve and Heart Health and directs the ValvulaR Multi-Imaging (VaRMI) core laboratory.

She has published more than 350 scientific articles and, since 2025, has been listed among the world's top 2% most-cited scientists in the Stanford/Elsevier ranking.

Read the full biography

The overall objective of her research program is to characterize sex differences in the pathophysiology, diagnosis and treatment of valvular heart diseases. To reach this objective, her laboratory conducts multidisciplinary, patient-oriented clinical research as well as basic science research, in order to develop and validate sex-specific strategies for the evaluation, management and treatment of patients with valvular heart disease.

In addition to her articles, she has presented more than 500 abstracts at national and international congresses. She has received several awards, including the 2019 Young Investigator Award and the 2024 Mid-Career Lecturer Award in cardiovascular sciences from the Canadian Cardiovascular Society and the Canadian Institutes of Health Research.

Dr. Clavel is a fellow of the Canadian Cardiovascular Society, the American Heart Association and the American College of Cardiology. She is a member of the board of directors of the Canadian Society of Echocardiography, Research Director on the board of the Heart Valve Society, and a senior associate editor of the European Heart Journal – Cardiovascular Imaging.
